This research investigates urban habitats of *Anopheles stephensi*, a malaria vector adapted to man-made water containers such as construction pits.

The project combines satellite image analysis, habitat and productivity prediction, and geospatial support for on-site larval surveys. It aims to help field teams prioritize surveillance and intervention.

The work is described on the original site as part of a project funded by the Gates Foundation.
