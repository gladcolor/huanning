A 2025 preprint presents a research approach to estimating hourly population at the neighborhood scale using mobile phone data.

An accompanying public map lets visitors explore geographic patterns, including selected events and locations.

[Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01170) · [Explore the interactive population map](https://gladcolor.github.io/hourly_population/).
