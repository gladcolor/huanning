This research reconstructs how neighborhood-level populations vary throughout the day using human mobility data. The 2022 dataset covers roughly 220,000 U.S. census block groups over 8,760 hours.

The accompanying public map lets visitors explore hourly population changes in 2D and 3D, including selected events and places.

Potential applications include environmental exposure assessment, emergency response, and studies of urban resilience.
