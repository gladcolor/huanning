I am a computational data scientist working on geospatial AI and AI for science. In 2023 I proposed **autonomous GIS**: AI agents that find geospatial data, run spatial analysis, and make maps from plain-language requests. I build these agents, including LLM-Geo, LLM-Find, and GIS Copilot.

I also apply spatial data and machine learning to public health and cities, from hourly population estimates for U.S. neighborhoods to mosquito habitat prediction for malaria control. Before research, I spent more than ten years in the geospatial industry as an engineer, developer, manager, and chief technology officer.
