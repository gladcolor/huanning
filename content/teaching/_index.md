I love sharing my knowledge and skills with others, and I am always happy to help others with my expertise. During my graduate studies, I taught college students as an independent instructor and as a teaching assistant. When I worked in the geospatial industry, I often held technical workshops and presentations to help colleagues improve their productivity.

This year I have been building lessons with AI for two levels of students: [Addiction in 3D](https://addictions.win/) for first-year college biology, including [The Dopamine Machine](../project/dopamine-machine/) and [Guard the Dopamine Factory](../project/guard-the-factory/), and [Genius Classroom (天才课堂)](../project/genius-classroom/), free bilingual middle-school lessons that read aloud. The posts below share my courses and my thoughts on education.
